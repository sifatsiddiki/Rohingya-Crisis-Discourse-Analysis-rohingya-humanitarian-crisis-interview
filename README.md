# Rohingya Humanitarian Crisis: Elite and Public Discourse in Bangladesh

##  Project Overview
This repository contains qualitative research documentation and interview transcripts focusing on the **Rohingya humanitarian crisis** and its evolution within elite and public discourses in Bangladesh. The study explores the shifting geopolitical, social, and economic factors influencing Bangladesh's response to the refugee influxes over time.

###  Key Research Insights Included
* **Historical Constraints (2012-2016):** Analysis of the internal political and domestic economic limitations that officially restricted Rohingya entry during this window.
* **The 2017 Shift:** Evaluation of the inevitable humanitarian response triggered by unprecedented atrocities in Rakhine State, heavily reinforced by shared religious and regional cultural/linguistic affinities.
* **Foreign Policy Implications:** Examination of how hosting refugees aligns with Bangladesh's objectives to emerge as a responsible regional actor and strengthen ties with global powers (China, India, Russia, and the USA).
* **Evolving Domestic Challenges:** Critical insights into why public and elite mindsets are shifting due to resource constraints, macroeconomic pressures, security concerns within camp perimeters, and social tensions.
